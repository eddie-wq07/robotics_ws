# Third-party foundations

- The tactile image/surface pipeline was developed/adapted by Sam and migrated from the team's `pen_testing/` project.
- The earlier robotics/viewer code adapted [gilwoolee/3d_art](https://github.com/gilwoolee/3d_art). Its MIT notice remains in [licenses/3d_art-MIT.txt](licenses/3d_art-MIT.txt); the retired implementation remains in Git history.
- The new runtime uses [Flexiv ROS 2](https://github.com/flexivrobotics/flexiv_ros2) and its robot description/RDK dependencies, installed separately under their upstream licenses. ROS 2, MoveIt 2, and RViz are also installed separately.
